01 · The problem

Every failed payment is a decision.

A failed transaction doesn't always mean lost revenue. The challenge is knowing which opportunity is worth pursuing, which action to use, and when to escalate.

⚠️

Revenue leakage

Failed payments can silently turn into permanent revenue loss.

🎯

One-size-fits-all recovery

Different customers and failures need different recovery strategies.

💸

Action cost matters

A higher predicted probability isn't automatically the most valuable action.

⏱️

Timing matters

Recovery teams need prioritization instead of an endless list of failed payments.
